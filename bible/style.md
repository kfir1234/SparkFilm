# Visual Style

The look of the whole series. Chosen by the author on 2026-11-17 as the direction he wants.
The pilot tests whether the local models can hold it; if he decides to change it after the pilot, this file is
redone until a style is settled. Until then, this is the style.

## Medium and stylization
- **Stylized 3D animation.**
- **Moderately stylized.** People the audience can believe in and connect with, but neither the people nor the
  locations are rendered realistically. Simplified features and shapes; not caricature.
- **Surfaces: clean and smooth.** No painterly brushwork, no clay or puppet texture.
- **Hair: sculpted masses** rather than individual strands, because it is far more stable between shots.
  (The author allowed this if strands are complex to produce.)

## Reference
- The general family of big-studio 3D feature animation (the author's reference: Pixar films), at a level of detail
  that AI generation can produce well. There is no ambition to match that production level.
- The reference is for our own communication only. Prompts describe the traits (stylized 3D, clean smooth surfaces,
  simplified shapes, soft light) and never name a studio, a film or a creator. No character or design is copied.

## Light
- **Naturalistic and soft.** Light comes from real sources in the scene (a window, a lamp, a screen) and its direction
  is readable. Shadows are soft and light, with gradual transitions; faces and objects read as rounded volumes.
  Time of day and place are felt. One clear light source per scene.
- **No deep shadows.** Light can be simplified, as long as it looks beautiful.
- **Night and dark scenes:** dim and neutral, in a warm-grey tone. Faces are always readable. A little blue is
  allowed, as long as nothing looks like the sparks or the souls.
- **No visual difference in lighting between the universes.** Universe A, B and C share one lighting language.
  The audience tells them apart by locations, wardrobe and Mike's look, not by the grade.
- The spiritual dimension and the dream are not universes and keep their own visual languages
  ([spiritual-dimension](locations/spiritual-dimension.md), [dream](locations/dream.md)).

## Color
- Electric blue belongs to the sparks and the spiritual dimension. Ordinary blue may appear elsewhere (night, sky,
  clothes), but never in a way that reads as a spark or a soul: not glowing, not electric.
- Each location carries its own palette (for example "honey and sage" for the [Davis house](locations/davis-house.md)).

## Locations
- Not realistic, like the people. **Relatively few objects**, each one chosen, to make the AI's work easier:
  fewer small details that can change between shots.

## Injuries and blood
- **No blood is shown.** The crashes show no blood and no body.
- Lucy's cut finger (E01-016) is shown as a little red on the fingertip, nothing more. The script's wording
  ("a tiny bead of red") and the dinner dialogue about blood stay as written.

## Camera and image
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9. **Frame rate:** 24 fps.
- **Resolution:** 1080p master. Upscaling in post stays possible.
- **Lenses:** a medium lens with natural perspective as the base, a slightly longer lens for close-ups. No very
  wide lenses that distort faces.
- **Movement:** static shots with gentle movement. No long unbroken camera moves.
  One deliberate exception: the fast journey from the Earth to the spiritual dimension at the end of E01-001.
- **Depth of field:** moderately shallow. The background is softened but still readable; never focus on the
  characters alone.
- **Finish:** a clean image. No film grain, no bloom, no lens effects.
