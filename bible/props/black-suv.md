# Black Sedan

Universe B. Mike's car as a startup CEO: sleek, expensive, black. The opposite of the
[silver company sedan](company-sedan.md).

- Seen in the EP01 cold open (E01-001): it tumbles into the empty intersection, already rolling over, and comes to
  rest on its roof in the middle. One wheel keeps turning. One hubcap rolls away. The collision is never seen.
- Rule: never show Mike inside it in the cold open.
