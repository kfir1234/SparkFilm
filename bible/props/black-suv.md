# Black SUV

Universe B. Mike's car as a startup CEO: a **large luxury SUV**, black (author's decision, 2026-11-18).
The opposite of the [silver company sedan](company-sedan.md).

- Seen in the EP01 cold open (E01-001): it tumbles into the empty intersection, already rolling over, and comes to
  rest on its roof in the middle. Its one lit headlight flickers and dies. The collision is never seen.
  (The turning wheel and the rolling hubcap were cut by the author on 2026-11-18.)
- Rule: never show Mike inside it in the cold open.

## Look (proposed, not locked)
- Tall, long and imposing, with a boxy, upright body, a flat roofline and big wheels. Glossy black paint.
- No real make, badge or logo.
- After the crash: roof edges crushed, panels dented, all windows shattered to opaque so nothing inside is seen,
  one headlight still on. It flickers and goes out in E01-S001-SH030 (author, 2026-11-18); from then on the wreck
  has no lights on.
