# Match-3 Game (on Mike's phone)

Universe A. The mobile game Mike plays compulsively at work. A fictional match-3: no real game's name, art or sounds.

- A grid of glossy, candy-colored gems. Swiping lines up three; chains explode in a shower of light.
- `LEVEL COMPLETE!` flashes and fades, then the next level.
- A cheerful "Level complete!" jingle. It is the sound bridge out of the title card into E01-003.
- Mike's phone also carries the [lock-screen selfie](lock-screen-selfie.md) and clips into the car's dashboard mount.
