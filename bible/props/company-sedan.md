# Company Sedan (silver)

Universe A. Mike's car: a silver company sedan, identical to every other car in the Halloran Systems garage.
Its meaning: his life as one more copy in a row. The contrast is the [black sedan](black-sedan.md) of Universe B.

## Exterior
- Silver. The same small company logo on every door.
- Unlocks with a key fob: chirps (`BEEP-BEEP`) and flashes its lights.

## Interior (E01-009, E01-010, E01-012)
- Push-button start. The dashboard lights up.
- A phone mount on the dashboard.
- A center screen with a map and a `HOME` button; it draws a blue route line. It also controls the music.

## Seen in
E01-006, E01-008 to E01-014.

## Open
- Model shape (no real make or badge). The Halloran Systems logo.
- The premise has a family photo in the car. EP01 does not show one.
