# Halloran Systems

Universe A. Mike's workplace: a large, anonymous tech company (the fictional stand-in for the premise's IBM).
Its look should say "one more copy in a row": the opposite of the Davis house.

## Areas seen in EP01

### Open office (E01-003, E01-005)
- Cubicles with low walls (Liam leans over one). Mike's desk: a monitor, keyboard, mouse, a chair that spins.
- Liam's cubicle is on Mike's way to the elevators.
- Day in E01-003, late-afternoon light in E01-005.

### Elevator (E01-005, E01-007)
- Buttons inside, a floor-number display. Opens directly onto the office floor and onto each garage level.

### Parking garage, levels 3 and 4 (E01-006, E01-008, E01-009)
- Concrete. Rows and rows of identical silver sedans: same model, same color, the same small company logo on every door.
- Level signs painted large on the pillars: `P3`, `P4`. The two levels look exactly the same.
- A ramp leads down and out to evening light. Mike's car is on level 4.
