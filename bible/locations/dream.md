# The Dream

Sarah's dream of Mike's family saying goodbye (E01-019). The audience believes it is Mike's dream until E01-020.

## Look
- A void of soft, warm light. Colors bleed like wet paint.
- Psychedelic and warm: the colors of the Davis house, bleeding and melting.
- **Not** the blue electricity of the spiritual dimension. The two must never be confused.

## Staging
- Katie, Joe and Lucy stand close together, the way they sat at dinner (E01-017).
- Each waves in their own way: Katie with a sad, gentle smile; Joe with his hand flick; Lucy with her band-aid finger.
- Mike is visible. He reaches for them and never gets closer.
- The reveal: they haven't moved. Mike is the one drifting backward and upward, away from them.

## Rules
- Cut straight in from Mike asleep (E01-018), with no transition effect that marks it as someone else's dream.

## Open
- Sound. Whether this visual language returns for Sarah's later visions.
