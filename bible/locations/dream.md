# The Dream

Sarah's dream of Mike's family saying goodbye (E01-021). The audience believes it is Mike's dream until E01-022.

## Look
- A void of soft, warm light. Colors bleed like wet paint.
- Psychedelic and warm: the colors of the Davis house, bleeding and melting. The house palette is "honey and sage"
  (cream, sage green, light oak, terracotta, mustard, rust): see [davis-house](davis-house.md).
- **Not** the blue electricity of the spiritual dimension. The two must never be confused.

## Staging
- Katie, Joe and Lucy stand close together, the way they sat at dinner (E01-017): seen from Mike, Lucy on the left,
  Katie in the middle, Joe on the right.
- Each waves in their own way: Katie with a sad, gentle smile; Joe with his hand flick; Lucy with her band-aid finger.
- Mike is visible. He reaches for them and never gets closer.
- The reveal: they haven't moved. Mike is the one drifting backward and upward, away from them.

## Rules
- Cut straight in from Mike asleep (E01-020), with no transition effect that marks it as someone else's dream.
