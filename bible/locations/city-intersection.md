# City Intersection

A quiet city intersection with traffic lights. It appears twice in EP01, and the two appearances are framed to rhyme.

## E01-001: Universe B, night (the second death)
- Wet asphalt reflecting the traffic lights. A light pole the black sedan ends up against.
- After the crash: a police cruiser and an ambulance at the edge of frame, red and blue light.
- Rules: never show Mike inside the car or the moment of impact up close. Keep accident vs. choice ambiguous.
  Katie B must not appear. No crowd.

## E01-011: Universe A, evening (the near-miss)
- Same angles as the cold open: the sedan entering, headlights from the side.
- The silver company sedan instead of the black one. No music, street sound only.
- The other car races to beat its own yellow and crosses inches from Mike's bumper.
