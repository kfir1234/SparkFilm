# Davis House

Universe A. The family home: a modest two-story house on a quiet suburban street. The warmest location in the film.
Recurring, so the layout must stay identical between the pilot, EP01 and later episodes.

## Areas seen in EP01

### Street and driveway (E01-013, E01-014)
- Quiet streets, trimmed lawns, kids' bikes in the driveways. Evening.
- A driveway in front of the house and a path to the front door.

### Living room (E01-015)
- Front door opens into it. A bowl for keys and a hook for jackets by the door.
- Couch facing a TV. Joe plays here.

### Kitchen (E01-016, pilot) — locked 2026-11-17
Script: a stove with a pot, an oven with a window, a counter with room for a step stool, and a kitchen table.

**Layout** (top view; the camera's main position is the open side):

```
                    BACK WALL
  +------------------------------------------------+
  | FRIDGE |  STOVE + OVEN  | COUNTER (step stool) |--> laundry door
  |                                                |
  |                                             SINK
doorway                                        WINDOW  (golden light)
(living room)                                      |
  |      KITCHEN TABLE                             |
  |      (Lucy draws)                              |
  +--------------------  open side  ---------------+
              main camera; opening to the dining nook
```

- Back wall: fridge, stove with the oven under it, and a counter continuing to the right. The stove and the step-stool
  position are two steps apart and fit in one frame.
- Right wall: the sink, with the window above it.
- Left wall: the doorway to the living room (Mike enters here).
- Back right corner: the door to the laundry room (Katie exits here; never seen inside).
- Foreground left: the kitchen table.
- Open side: the camera side, and the opening to the dining nook.

**Materials and colors ("honey and sage"):**
- Walls: warm cream. Cabinets: soft sage green with brass handles. Counter: light oak. Floor: terracotta tiles.
- Accents: mustard, rust, earthenware.
- Lived-in, not new: children's drawings and a calendar on the fridge, a fruit bowl, mismatched mugs.
- This palette is the base for the whole house and for [the dream](dream.md).

**Light:**
- Key: low evening sun through the right-wall window. Golden side light, long shadows falling to the left.
- Secondary: orange glow from the oven window; warm under-cabinet lights.
- Over the scene the light deepens from gold to amber. By dinner it is dark outside.
- Rule: the light always comes from the right.

### Dining nook (E01-017) — locked 2026-11-17
Script: a table for four, warm light, Katie carries the bread in from the kitchen.

**Layout** (top view):

```
            WALL WITH WINDOW  (dark outside)
  +------------------------------------------+
  |                  MIKE                    |
  |                                          |
  |        JOE    ( round    LUCY            |
  |                 table )                  |
  |                                          |
  | SIDEBOARD        KATIE                   |
  +----------  opening to the kitchen  ------+
```

- A round oak table for four.
- **Seating:** Mike with his back to the window, facing the kitchen. Katie opposite him, her back to the kitchen
  opening (she enters with the bread straight to her chair). Lucy on Mike's left, Joe on Mike's right.
- From Mike's chair the other three form an arc facing him, Katie in the middle. [The dream](dream.md) copies
  exactly this grouping.
- A sideboard with framed family photos on the left wall.

**Materials and colors:** the house palette. Cream walls, oak table and chairs, seat cushions in mustard and rust,
a sage-green sideboard like the kitchen cabinets, a pendant lamp with a natural woven shade.

**Light:**
- Key: the pendant lamp, low over the table. A warm pool of light; faces lit from above and slightly in front.
- The edges of the room fall into soft shadow.
- The window behind Mike: deep, muted night blue, far from the electric blue of the sparks.
- A little warm light spills in from the kitchen opening behind Katie.

### Master bedroom (E01-018)
- A bed with a nightstand and a lamp on Katie's side. An open door to the bathroom, where the sink is visible.
