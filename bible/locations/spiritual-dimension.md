# The Spiritual Dimension

Where souls live. Shown on screen (locked decision). First seen in the EP01 cold open (#E01-002#).

## Look (author's vision, 2026-11-13)
- Reached by tilting up from the night sky; the transition is tinted a deep **ember blue**, as if lit from inside.
- A vast network of souls, each a core of **blue electricity**, connected to each other by threads of current
  (all souls are connected; see [story/mythology.md](../../story/mythology.md)).
- **Sparks** constantly stream out of souls (into the universes) and back into them (at death).
- Abstract energy, no faces or bodies.

## Design (locked 2026-11-17)

**Structure of the space**
- A vertical axis: the souls are above, the dark is below. Sparks dive down and rise back up.
- The universes are never seen from here. Below the web there is only deep darkness.
- The web is endless: it runs in every direction and dissolves into haze. No edge, no center.
- Scale: a soul is large, a spark is tiny. In the wide shot the sparks read as glowing dust flowing between souls.

**A soul**
- A dense knot of threads of light converging on a core, with no clean outline. Organic, not a sphere.
- The threads of the knot run on outward and become the currents that join it to other souls.
- **All souls are equal**: the same size and the same brightness. None reads as greater or more evolved.

**Palette: blue with an ember**
- Background: deep blue-black. All the light is electric blue.
- Deep inside each soul's core there is a small amber ember, as if it were lit from inside.
- The amber stays inside the cores. It never becomes a light source for the space, so the dimension still reads
  as blue and is never confused with the warm [dream](dream.md).
- The ember-blue tint of the transition from the night sky and of the title card belongs to the same family.

**Sparks**
- Small points, brighter and whiter than the souls, with a short tail. They travel in streams along the currents.
- **In E01-002 all sparks look alike, including Mike's.** Slight natural variation between sparks is fine, but nothing
  marks Mike's out; only the camera follows it.

**Light and motion**
- No outside light source. Everything is lit by the souls themselves.
- Two rhythms: the web pulses slowly, like breathing; the sparks are fast and sharp.
- The BOOM: a white-blue flash that fills the frame, then total black.

**Fixed shots**

| | Shot | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| A | Extreme wide, slow drift | The whole web |
| B | Medium on one soul | Sparks leaving and diving |
| C | Wide, looking up from below | Sparks returning and sinking in |
| D | Tracking one rising spark | Mike's journey |
| E | Close-up | The spark hits its soul |

## Rules for using it
- Never show where Mike's spark goes after the second death (protects the Universe C twist).
- Never show the universes from inside the dimension.
