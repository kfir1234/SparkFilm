# Mike Davis

Protagonist. 35, the same age as Katie (decided 2026-11-17). Facts below come from the script; anything not yet decided is under Open.

## Universe A (EP01)
- Junior developer at Halloran Systems. Sharp, fast, and bored: finishes his work early and coasts.
- Married to Katie; father of Joe (10) and Lucy (6).
- Plays a match-3 game on his phone compulsively.
- Leaves work at 5:00 PM on the dot. Drives a silver company sedan, one of many identical ones.
- Feels he "missed something", that he was "supposed to be somewhere else", and can't explain it.
- At home: warm, present, playful. Cooks alongside Katie, hums while he works, snores the moment he lies down.
- Watches Katie tuck in the sleeping kids from the doorway (E01-019) and tells her, in a whisper and without a joke,
  that she's an amazing mom.

## Universe B
- Startup CEO, rich, engaged to Jennie. Left Katie after college. Drives a sleek black sedan.
- In the EP01 cold open he is heard (V.O.) but never seen.

## Personality and voice
- Deadpan humor, quick comebacks ("Define 'touched.'"). Deflects with jokes, including about himself.
- Speaks in short sentences. Repeats words when shaken ("Okay. Okay. I'm okay.").
- Narration (V.O.): slow, quiet, wry. Frustrated, yet at peace with death.

## Look (decided 2026-11-17)
The same face in both universes; grooming, posture and wardrobe tell them apart.

- **Build:** average height, slightly slumped shoulders, the beginning of softness at the belly.
- **Face:** soft features, thick expressive eyebrows, a crooked smile.
- **Hair:** dark brown.
  - Universe A: neat and combed, medium length (not too short).
  - Universe B: neat, and cut shorter.
- **Facial hair:** Universe A two-day stubble. Universe B clean-shaven.
- **Body in Universe B:** the same frame, trimmer and more upright.
- **Universe C:** he looks like Universe A Mike (decided 2026-11-17).
- **Silhouette:** deliberately ordinary; he blends in. What identifies him without his face is his **posture**:
  slumped shoulders in Universe A, upright in Universe B. No added physical mark.

- **Eyes:** brown (electric blue is reserved for the sparks).
- **No glasses.**
- **Wedding ring** in Universe A, none in Universe B.

## LoRA (decided 2026-11-17)
- Trigger word: `spkmike`.
- **One LoRA for all universes.** It learns the identity (face, eyebrows, smile, eyes, proportions). What differs
  between universes (stubble, hair length, posture, wardrobe) is written in every training caption and in the prompt.
- The first training set includes both the Universe A and the Universe B look, in balanced numbers, so the face
  is locked once for the whole series. Universe C uses the Universe A look and needs no extra training.
- Split into separate LoRAs only if tests show the looks bleeding into each other.
