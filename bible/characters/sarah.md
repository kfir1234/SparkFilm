# Sarah

The medium. 52 (decided 2026-11-17). Facts below come from the script and [story/open-questions.md](../../story/open-questions.md).

- First seen at the end of EP01 (E01-022): she wakes with a gasp from the "family goodbye" dream, drenched in a
  cold sweat, in a bed and a room we have never seen.
- The audience assumes the dream is Mike's until her lamp comes on.
- She dreams **about** Mike: he is visible in the dream (E01-021).
- Her only line in EP01: "Oh no." (whispered).

## Rules for EP01
- Keep her face and room dark until the lamp clicks on.

## Look (decided 2026-11-17)
- **Build:** slightly full. Shoulders drawn in.
- **Skin:** dark.
- **Hair:** white, very long (to her waist), not groomed. Loose at home and in bed; in a single braid when she is
  out of the house.
- **Face:** tired. Dark circles under her eyes: she has not slept well in years.
- **Eyes:** dark brown.
- No "medium" accessories: no scarves, crystals or heavy jewelry. She looks like a real person.
