# Joe Davis

Mike and Katie's son. 10. Facts below come from the script; anything not yet decided is under Open.

## Universe A (EP01)
- Plays an online video game on the living-room TV with a headset on; doesn't hear his father come in.
- Got an A on the science test.
- Answers in one or two words ("Fine." / "Fine good." / "Busted.") until he's drawn out.

## Personality and voice
- A little too cool for his parents, but grins despite himself. Laughs with the family in the end.

## Signature gesture
- **The hand flick** (E01-015): without taking his eyes off the screen, he leans around Mike and flicks a hand.
  Half "hi, Dad", half "you're in the way". He repeats the same flick as his goodbye in the dream (E01-019).
  The two must be recognizably the same gesture.
