# Katie Davis

Mike's wife in Universe A. 35, the same age as Mike (decided 2026-11-17). Facts below come from the script; anything not yet decided is under Open.

## Universe A (EP01)
- Dentist ("Twelve mouths and one of them actually bit me").
- Mother of Joe (10) and Lucy (6). Runs the household logistics: dinner, laundry, the birthday party.
- Bakes bread. Cooks chicken and potatoes in EP01.
- About to leave for a conference on new technologies in dentistry. Lucy's birthday is the day after she returns.
- Checks on the sleeping kids before bed (E01-019). Brushes off Mike's compliment: "Stop with the nonsense."
- First seen in E01-016. Also in the dream (E01-021), waving goodbye with a sad, gentle smile.

## Universe B

- Must **not** appear in the EP01 cold open.

## Personality and voice
- Dry, deadpan humor that matches Mike's ("Dentists are only allowed to wash their hands once a week").
- Affectionate through teasing: throws a pillow, "Wonder where she gets it."
- Practical and direct. Short sentences.
- Tender when unobserved: strokes Mike's hair while he snores.

## Look (decided 2026-11-17)

- **Face:** angular, prominent cheekbones, a direct gaze.

- **Eyes:** soft grey-blue. A natural eye color, clearly not the electric blue of the sparks, which stays
  the only glowing blue in the film.
