# EP01 Scene-by-Scene Breakdown

Per scene: who is in it and what they wear, where everyone stands and moves, what the set contains, and the props.
Shots are in [../shotlists/ep01.csv](../shotlists/ep01.csv). The episode summary is in [ep01.md](ep01.md).

**How to read it.** Anything marked **(P)** is a proposal by Claude and is **not canon** until the author approves it.
Everything unmarked comes from the script. Approved proposals move into `bible/`.

Screen directions (left, right) are from the camera's point of view.

## E01-001 — EXT. CITY INTERSECTION - NIGHT (Universe B)

- **Characters:** none seen. Mike is voice only (V.O.). Emergency crews as silhouettes at most.
- **Set contains:** a four-way intersection, wet asphalt, traffic lights on each corner, streetlights, one light pole
  on the far-right corner that the sedan ends against. Dark, unbranded storefronts **(P)**. No pedestrians, no other traffic.
- **Props and vehicles:** black sedan; the car that hits it (dark, never seen clearly **(P)**); one hubcap; police cruiser;
  ambulance; broken glass on the asphalt.
- **Staging:** the black sedan enters from frame left and crosses to the right **(P)**. The other car comes from the back of
  frame toward camera and hits its far side **(P)**. The sedan spins and stops against the pole, driver's side away from
  camera **(P)**, so the interior is never visible. The cruiser and ambulance stop at the left edge of frame.
- **Light:** night, cool streetlight, traffic-light colors in the wet road, then red and blue flashes. Ends on a sky that
  glows ember blue.
- **Sound:** crash, silence, the hubcap, then distant sirens. V.O. only after the crash.
- **Continuity out:** the camera rises into the sky and into E01-002.

## E01-002 — THE SPIRITUAL DIMENSION

- **Characters:** none. Abstract energy, no faces or bodies.
- **Set contains:** a vast web of souls, each a core of blue electricity, joined by threads of current. Sparks stream out
  and down into the dark; others stream back up.
- **Staging:** one spark is followed upward until it hits its soul. Do not show where it goes afterward.
- **Then:** black for two seconds, the title card, and the game jingle as a sound bridge.

## E01-003 — INT. HALLORAN SYSTEMS - OPEN OFFICE - DAY

- **Time:** late morning **(P)** (Mike finished the module "this morning"; lunch follows).
- **Characters and wardrobe:** Mike (MIKE-1, jacket on the back of his chair), Jeff (JEFF-1), Liam (LIAM-1).
- **Set contains:** rows of low-walled cubicles in grey and beige; fluorescent ceiling light; carpet tiles.
  Mike's cubicle: an L-shaped desk, one monitor, keyboard, mouse, a swivel chair, a mug, a small framed family photo turned
  slightly away **(P)**, a stack of untouched printouts **(P)**. Liam's cubicle shares Mike's left wall **(P)**.
- **Props:** Mike's phone with the match-3 game; mouse; monitor graphics (screensaver, test dashboard).
- **Staging:** Mike sits facing his desk with his back to the aisle, phone held low in both hands. Jeff stands in the aisle
  directly behind him, arms crossed. Mike drops the phone face-down and spins the chair to face Jeff, so Mike looks up and
  Jeff looks down for the whole exchange. Jeff points past Mike's shoulder at the monitor, then leans in over the desk to
  scroll. Jeff exits down the aisle to frame right **(P)**, calling "Thursday!" over his shoulder. Mike turns back, picks up
  the phone and leans back. Liam rises over the left cubicle wall; Mike stands and they leave to frame right **(P)**, toward
  the elevators.
- **Light:** flat, cool office light. The only warm color is the game on the phone, on Mike's face.
- **Sound:** the "Level complete!" jingle bridges in from black; game sounds; office hum.

## E01-004 — INT. LUCA'S BISTRO - DAY

- **Characters and wardrobe:** Mike (MIKE-1, no jacket **(P)**: the bistro is a short walk), Liam (LIAM-1), Olivia (OLIVIA-1).

  sun; wooden chairs, checked or plain paper table covers **(P)**; a chalkboard menu with no readable brand names **(P)**;

- **Sound:** quiet room tone, a few dishes. No music sting on the lock screen.

## E01-016 — INT. DAVIS HOUSE - KITCHEN - CONTINUOUS

- **Characters and wardrobe:** Mike (MIKE-3, towel from mid-scene), Katie (KATIE-1), Lucy (LUCY-1, no band-aid yet).
- **Set contains:** script: a stove with a pot, an oven with a window, a counter with room for a step stool, a kitchen
  table. Layout **(P)**: stove and oven on the back wall; the counter continues to the right of the stove, two steps away, so
  the stool position and the stove are in one frame; the table in the foreground left; a window over the sink on the right
  wall giving the golden light; a doorway on the left to the living room and a door at the back right to the laundry room.
  Dressing **(P)**: children's drawings and a calendar on the fridge, a fruit bowl, a spice rack, mismatched mugs.
- **Props:** pot with a wooden spoon; bread in the oven; dish towel; step stool; small cutting board; kid's knife;
  cucumber; salad bowl **(P)**; Lucy's drawing and crayons on the table.
- **Staging, arrival:** Katie at the stove with her back to the room; Lucy at the table. Mike enters from the left doorway.
  Lucy runs to him across the foreground; he lifts her, sets her down, crosses to Katie at the stove and kisses her. They
  talk side by side at the stove. Katie hands him the towel and exits back right. Mike puts the step stool at the counter to
  the right of the stove; Lucy climbs up. He hands her the board, knife and cucumber, then turns back left to the stove.
- **Staging, pilot sequence:** Mike at the stove, facing the back wall, stirring. Lucy on the stool to his right, also facing
  the wall. She holds the cucumber with her left hand and cuts with her right; she nicks the tip of her **left index
  finger (P)**. Mike turns right, two steps, crouches to her eye level on her left side, takes her left hand, kisses the
  fingertip, then cups her cheeks. She hugs him; the last shot is on her face over his left shoulder **(P)**.
- **Light:** golden evening light from the right-hand window; warm oven glow.
- **Sound:** pot simmering, Mike's humming, then crying. Katie and Joe do not react: she is in the laundry room, he has
  his headset on.
- **Continuity out:** the cut finger gets the star band-aid before dinner.

  stops; Katie puts down her fork. Lucy holds her finger up three times in the scene.
- **Light:** warm pendant light, faces lit from above and slightly in front.
- **Continuity out:** this seating is what the dream copies.
