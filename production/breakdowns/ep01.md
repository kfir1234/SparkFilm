## Continuity to hold

- Mike: jacket on at work and in the car, hung by the front door in scene 15, off for the rest of the evening.
  Dish towel over his shoulder from the middle of scene 16.
- Lucy: band-aid from scene 17 on, asleep in scene 19, and in the dream.
- Mike and Katie: night clothes in scenes 18–20. The kids in pajamas in scene 19, but in their day clothes in the dream. Same finger as the cut in scene 16.
- Joe: the hand flick in scene 15 and in the dream is the same gesture.
- The dream groups Katie, Joe and Lucy "the way they sat at dinner": match the seating of scene 17.
- Scene 11 is framed to rhyme with scene 1: same angles, silver sedan instead of black.
- Katie's hands: she is a dentist and has just baked bread. No band-aids or marks.
