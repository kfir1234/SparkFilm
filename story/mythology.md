# Mythology: Rules of Sparks, Souls and Universes

Canon as defined by the author. Every scene must stay consistent with these rules.
Proposals that haven't been decided go to [open-questions.md](open-questions.md), not here.

## Core rules

1. **A soul lives in a spiritual dimension.** What lives inside a human is a **spark**: one part of that soul.
2. **Every major decision splits the universe.** The new universe is populated by **new sparks from the same souls**,
   so every person in every universe holds a spark from the same soul as their counterparts.
3. **At death, the spark returns to its soul**, which processes what it learned (mistakes and achievements).
   The soul's purpose is to advance and evolve through its sparks.
4. **The anomaly:** when Mike dies in Universe A, a mistake happens. When the next universe (B) is created, every person in it
   gets a new spark **except Mike**: his body receives his **old, recently collected spark** instead of a new one.
   Universe B is **not a branch of A**. It is newly created, but its past diverges from A's (Mike left Katie after college
   and founded the startup).
5. **New universes are created with a full past.** Universe B is new, yet it has years of history that its people remember.
6. **Two kinds of memory:** the **body/brain** remembers the current universe's past (Mike gradually "remembers" Jennie and
   the B life). The **spark** carries the previous life (Katie, Lucy, Joe, the crash).
7. **The twist:** when Mike dies in Universe B, he does **not** go back to Universe A. His spark goes into a **third, newly
   created universe (C)** with its own past. It is only implied on screen: a baby he never had.
