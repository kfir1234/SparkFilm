# Motifs

Recurring lines, sounds and images. Their exact wording is canon: keep it identical every time it returns.

## The ritual ("Like the sun loves the sky")

The film's emotional spine. First heard in EP01 (#E01-016#, the pilot sequence).

Canonical wording:

```
MIKE
Lucy, don't cry. Do you know how much I love you? Do you?

LUCY
How much, Daddy?

MIKE
Like the sun loves the sky.

LUCY
And will you always love me, Daddy?

MIKE
Always always!
```

- In EP01 it continues with a light coda that shows it's an everyday ritual, not a one-time moment:
  LUCY: "I love it when you tell me how much you love me." / MIKE: "I know you do. I love it too."
- **Film ending (decided 2026-11-14):** the ritual stops at "Always always!". No coda.
